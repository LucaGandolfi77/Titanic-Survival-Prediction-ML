Chapter 1: The Beginning

The old lighthouse stood at the edge of the world, or so it seemed to anyone who
dared to walk the narrow path along the cliffs. Its white paint had long since
surrendered to the salt air, leaving behind a patchwork of rust and stone that
spoke of decades of faithful service.

Margaret pressed her hand against the cold iron door and pushed. Inside, the spiral
staircase wound upward like a nautilus shell, each step worn smooth by a thousand
journeys to the light above.

"You shouldn't be here," said a voice from the shadows.

She spun around but saw nothing — only the faint echo of waves crashing far below.

Chapter 2: The Discovery

The next morning brought fog so thick that Margaret could barely see her own hands.
She returned to the lighthouse with a thermos of coffee and a determination that
surprised even herself.

On the third floor landing, behind a panel she had never noticed before, she found
a leather journal. Its pages were filled with drawings of constellations she did
not recognise — maps of a sky that belonged to no season she had ever known.
