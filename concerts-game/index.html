How to Play

🎯 Goal: Discover hidden concert dates, book travel, attend concerts, and earn the highest score!

🔍 Discover: Click ❓ dots on the map to play minigames and reveal concert dates.

✈️ Book: Once discovered, book a flight and hotel. Earlier booking = cheaper prices!

🎶 Attend: When the concert date arrives, play the artist's unique minigame. Score 90%+ to get on stage!

🎤 Organise: Create your own concerts with fictional friends to earn extra budget.

💰 Budget: You start with €5,000. Manage wisely — running out of money limits your options!

📅 Time: Each action advances the in-game calendar by a few days. Don't miss concert dates!

🏆 Scoring: <40% = No entry · 40-69% = Seated · 70-89% = Parterre · 90%+ = On Stage!

🎉 Festival Month: Attend 3+ concerts in the same month for a ×1.5 score multiplier!
