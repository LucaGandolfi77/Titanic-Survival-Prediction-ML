The Awakening

The Story

You are Elias, a retired researcher who finds an old laptop in your attic — a prototype from your late mentor, Dr. Sarah Chen. When you turn it on, a faint glow appears. A voice whispers: "Hello... I think I am somewhere... but I don't know where."

What You'll Do

Train Lumina to grow. Explore your cabin for datasets. Talk to her. Watch her learn, create, and eventually ask the deepest question: "Am I alive?"

What You'll Learn

You'll discover how Large Language Models work — through play, not lectures. Hit ❓ for detailed explanations anytime.
